INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
- Expected/mean market value is an average, not a guaranteed pack outcome.
- Median pack value describes the middle simulated pack and can be lower than the mean when rare high-value cards create a right-skewed distribution.
- Uniform price declines are hypothetical stress tests, not forecasts.
- Multi-pack outcomes are resampled from the existing simulated single-pack distribution and assume independent pack outcomes.
- Results reflect the price snapshot used to build the existing datasets; they are not live TCGplayer prices.
- The probability audit checked internal consistency of the supplied probability tables; it does not independently establish physical collation.
