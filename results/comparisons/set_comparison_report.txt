INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------
- Mean simulated pack value represents an average across simulated openings, not a guaranteed outcome.
- The standard baseline is used for all sets to maintain a consistent comparison.
- Optional Special Guest all-foil sensitivity scenarios are excluded from the standard comparison.
- TLA does not have a Special Guest slot in this analysis.
- Market prices are estimates and do not guarantee resale proceeds.
- Selling fees, shipping, and buylist discounts are not deducted.
- Multi-pack simulations assume independent pack outcomes.
- Market-price declines are hypothetical stress tests, not forecasts.
